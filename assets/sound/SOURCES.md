# OpenCiv2's own sound effects

Played where the game folder lacks the original's `Sound\NAME.WAV` (src/platform/sound.c:
`sound_fallback`; docs/NO-INSTALL.md): one file a sound, `name.ogg` for `NAME.WAV`.  Every source
is in the public domain ([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)): no attribution is required; their authors are
credited here all the same.  Sources: [Freesound](https://freesound.org) (each sound's licence
checked on its page: Creative Commons 0; the site's high-quality preview used),
[OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org) (CC0 items), [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) (CC0 packs) and
sgossner's [VSCO-2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE) orchestral samples (CC0).
Two are composed for OpenCiv2 rather than recorded: `fanfare3.ogg` and `fanfare5.ogg`, sequenced
from single notes of sgossner's CC0 VSCO-2 samples (on Freesound and GitHub).

Each was made from its sources with ffmpeg: mixed where there are several, leading and trailing
silence trimmed, cut to about the original's length (faded out; the loops not).  Then, for all
alike, a gain matching its loudness to the original's (RMS over the louder half of its 50 ms
windows, within ±0.5 dB) and a true-peak limiter to −1 dBFS (4x oversampled; at most 3 dB of
limiting, 3.5 dB where that reached the original's level: a few stay quieter than the original,
as noted), the loops processed as loops so their seam is unchanged; decoded as floating point and encoded once: 22050 Hz mono (the
game reads 22050 Hz 8-bit WAV: sound.c converts), Ogg Vorbis at quality 4.
